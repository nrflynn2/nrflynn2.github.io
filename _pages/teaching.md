## UC Berkeley CHEM 274B: Molecular Science & Software Engineering

**Adjunct Faculty** &mdash; Graduate-level course covering machine learning and cheminformatics for molecular science applications.

This course teaches software engineering practices, computational chemistry tools, and machine learning methods in the context of real molecular science problems. Students build hands-on experience with RDKit, PyTorch, and scientific Python while working through case studies from drug discovery and materials science.

- **Course repository:** [github.com/nrflynn2/swe-molecular-sciences](https://github.com/nrflynn2/swe-molecular-sciences)
- **Topics:** Python for scientists, RDKit and cheminformatics, molecular ML, graph neural networks, generative models for drug design
